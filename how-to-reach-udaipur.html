How to Reach Udaipur

Udaipur is well connected to major cities in India by air, rail, and road.

1. By Air

Udaipur is served by Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR), located approximately 22 km from the city centre.

For travel from the airport to Udaipur city, participants are advised to use the pre-paid taxi service available at the airport.

Bus shuttle services are also available from the airport to the city centre at regular intervals.

2. By Train

Udaipur is well connected by rail to several major cities in India, including Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, and others.

The main railway stations serving the city are:

  • Udaipur City Railway Station (UDZ) – the principal railway station
  • Rana Pratap Nagar Railway Station (RPZ) – another convenient railway station serving Udaipur

3. By Road

Udaipur is well connected by road to major cities in Rajasthan and neighbouring states. Regular state transport and private bus services, including luxury Volvo and BharatBenz buses, operate to and from Udaipur.

Frequent bus services are available to major cities such as Delhi, Ahmedabad, and Jaipur.

Local Transportation in Udaipur

Participants can use auto-rickshaws, taxis, and app-based transportation services for travel within Udaipur.

  • Rapido – generally more readily available in Udaipur
  • Uber – available for local transportation in Udaipur

Pre-paid taxi services are also available at Maharana Pratap Airport and can be used for convenient travel to the city.
